Sports & Hobbies

Team sport taught me leadership, and climbing and hiking keep me curious.

Volleyball

I've been playing volleyball for over 8 years, since joining my local team in Stafford, and I've been hugely passionate about the sport ever since. A few years in, my club paid for me to become a qualified referee, and I've refereed games for both the club and the university.

I made Swansea University's first team in my first year and was made captain in my second. That season we won the division and promotion. I was also elected Club Development Officer and ran a weekly intramural league to get new players into the sport.

In Hong Kong I joined a social club at CityU and met some great friends. Back in Swansea, I served as the club's Treasurer for the 2024/25 season and sat on the Students' Union Sports Executive Committee, helping run events such as Ladies' Day.

Bouldering

More recently I've become a climber. I first tried rock climbing when I was younger, through a £1-a-session club at my high school, and bouldering brought back how much I enjoy solving small physical problems.

In Hong Kong I made the most of a great student deal and climbed during the quieter hours. It was something I could do on my own or with friends, and a welcome break from the busy city and the humidity.

Hiking & outdoors

I rediscovered hiking in Hong Kong. It was a great way to meet like-minded students and explore my new home, and it gave me some amazing views, like reaching Lion Rock's peak just in time for sunset. A friend and I also set out to tackle the MacLehose Trail, which runs 100 km across the region.

I also got to go paddle boarding along Sai Kung's coast, and kayaking.
