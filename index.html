Welcome!

Hi, I'm Euan, co-founder and tech lead of Nomien. Nomien is a B2B platform that helps coffee roasters discover new café partners and sell their guest coffee, and it integrates with the systems roasters already run.

I build the whole platform myself. It's a .NET Razor Pages app on PostgreSQL, deployed to Google Cloud Run and Cloud SQL with Terraform. Each release goes through an automated pipeline that only sends traffic to the new version once its smoke tests pass. We're building Nomien through the Alacrity Foundation Entrepreneurship Programme, with 3 roasters ready to join our pilot and over 30 cafés lined up.

Before Nomien, I graduated with a First in Computer Science from Swansea University, including a year at CityU Hong Kong, and worked as a .NET developer intern at SeRP.
